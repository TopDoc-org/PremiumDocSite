About the Doctor

10+ Years of Expertise in Orthodontics & Dental Care

Dr. Jasbir Meher (BDS, MDS) is a renowned Orthodontist and Dental Surgeon based in Balangir, Odisha. He founded Dr. Meher's Orthodontic Center & Dental Clinic in 2015, bringing world-class orthodontic and dental care to the region.
